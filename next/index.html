    Walk to the station
    Put it on your home screen
    1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
    2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
    3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

    It opens straight to your station with live times, like an app. Times update every 30 seconds while it's open.

    Home screen widget

    A widget shows when to leave without opening anything. It uses Scriptable, a free app that lets you add your own widgets.

    1. Install Scriptable from the App Store (free).
    2. Tap Copy widget below. It's already set up for your station and walk time.
    3. Open Scriptable, tap + in the top corner, paste, and tap Done.
    4. On your home screen, press and hold an empty spot, tap Edit then Add Widget, choose Scriptable and the medium size, and tap Add Widget.
    5. Press and hold the new widget, tap Edit Widget, and set Script to the one you pasted.
    Copied

    The "leave in" countdown on the widget ticks by itself. iPhones only let widgets fetch new times every few minutes, so for up-to-the-second times tap the widget to open this page. If you change your station or walk time here, copy the widget again and paste it over the old one.
