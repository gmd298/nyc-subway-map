        About this map

        Stations, tracks and service patterns come from the MTA's published schedule data (typical weekday midday service). Shoreline from NYC Open Data and the US Census; parks and roads © OpenStreetMap contributors. When live data can be reached, trains, next-train times, service alerts and elevator status come straight from the MTA's public feeds; train positions are worked out from each train's due times at its next stops. Otherwise trains are a simulation. Landmark buildings and bridges are simplified models, enlarged a little so they are easy to see. Heights are exaggerated so tunnels and elevated tracks are easy to tell apart.
